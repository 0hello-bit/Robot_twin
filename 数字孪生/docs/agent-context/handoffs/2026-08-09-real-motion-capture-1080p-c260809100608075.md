## VERIFIED

- The canonical synchronized lifecycle sent `START`, observed `RUNNING`,
  collected telemetry and camera frames, sent `STOP`, observed matching
  `STOPPED/STOP`, and completed socket, camera, and reader cleanup.
- The live firmware identity was `fw_build_id=3` in the health frames.
- The run contains 166 camera frame records and 260 telemetry records. All
  camera frame records are `1920x1080`; frame timestamps have zero
  non-increasing pairs.
- The synchronization gate passed: coverage `100%`, p95 camera/telemetry
  time difference `15.294889 ms`, below the `33.333333 ms` limit.
- All 260 telemetry records reported `imu_init_status=0` and
  `imu_validity=0x0F`. This verifies the reported runtime validity fields for
  this run, not IMU yaw calibration, sign, drift, or control benefit.
- AprilTag observation failed independently: 90/166 detections (`54.2168%`),
  76 failures, all classified as `candidates_rejected`; no camera read
  failures or `no_markers` failures occurred.
- The observation report measured maximum pose gap `34.946622` frame periods
  and detector processing p95 `75.541850 ms` against a `33.333333 ms` budget.
- The existing additive motion artifact reports `REAL_SYNC` motion evidence,
  but it remains relative exploratory evidence because the selected 1080p
  profile is `EXPLORATORY_RELATIVE_ONLY`.

## INFERENCE

- The camera can resolve the tag during successful frames: observed successful
  tag side length was approximately `56.8-65.5 px`. Therefore the low ratio is
  not explained by a global 720p/low-resolution configuration error.
- The detector found candidate structures on every failed frame across its
  multi-scale and fallback attempts, but strict ID=0 validation did not accept
  them. Motion blur, oblique/partial tag views, illumination/reflection, or
  candidate geometry are plausible causes; this run cannot distinguish them
  without the failed images.
- The `75.5 ms` processing p95 is a verified throughput bottleneck that lowers
  the effective processed frame rate and creates long observation gaps. It is
  a separate cause from the per-frame candidate rejection decision.

## INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

- No continuous AprilTag observation pass, B3 observation pass, formal 2 mm
  ground holdout, absolute physical pose accuracy, wheel speed, or encoder
  evidence.
- No proof that the IMU yaw is calibrated or that camera/IMU fusion improves
  control. `fusion.jsonl` is an additive camera-anchored evidence stream.
- The 76 failed-frame thumbnails were not saved in this run: Windows
  `cv2.imwrite` returned false for the workspace's non-ASCII path. The raw
  frame index and failure counts are retained, but the visual cause cannot be
  reconstructed from this run.
